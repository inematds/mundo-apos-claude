💡 New here? Start here

O Claude is an artificial intelligence — the kind that chats, writes, and codes for you. Until yesterday, most people used AI only to ask a question. The world “after Claude” is different: AI has become a employee who executes — and your role is tell it what to do and check whether it turned out well. That’s what this course is about.

The shift: you become the conductor

Think of an orchestra. The conductor doesn’t play any instrument — it decides what to touch and when, and notices when something sounded wrong. It owns the result.

In the world after Claude, you are the conductor and AI is the orchestra. Building has become easy and cheap. What matters now is knowing what build, why, and check whether actually worked.

Your advantage: you don't need to program. Anyone who knows a real problem—from work or life—already has what matters most for directing AI. Experience is worth more than memorizing tools.
