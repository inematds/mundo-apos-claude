MODULE 1.3

🔌 Skills, MCP, and the Ecosystem

An AI on its own is like a brilliant brain locked in a room with no door. This module shows the pieces that give it arms and legs to it: the skills (recipes that teach the AI to work your way) and the MCP (the connector that plugs the AI into your email, calendar, and spreadsheets). It's what turns a standalone AI into a system that does things in the real world.

💡 New here? Three words before you start

  • Skill — a ready-made “recipe” you give the AI so it always does a task your way, exactly the same every time. It’s your knowledge packaged up.
  • MCP — a “universal plug” (the English acronym for Model Context Protocol) that connects AI to services like email, spreadsheets, and calendars without a workaround.
  • Connector — each “cable” that connects the AI to a specific service. The more connectors, the more things the AI can do.
1

📜 What Is a Skill

A skill (the English word for "skill") is a ready-made recipe that teaches AI to do a specific task — always from the your the way you do it, every time. It's like writing "how I put together my business proposal" on a piece of paper and giving that paper to the AI to follow every time.

Without a skill, you have to explain everything again in every conversation. With a skill, you packages your knowledge once and the AI repeats it faithfully — without forgetting a step, inventing a different format, or making you keep reminding it.

🔑 The central idea

Think about the difference between hire someone new every day e train a loyal employee:

  • •Without a skill: with every conversation, you explain everything again, and the result varies.
  • •With a skill: the AI already knows the steps, tone, and format—every time, the same way.

🧪 What a skill looks like on the inside (in simple terms)

Goal: see that a skill is just a little set of instructions you'd give the AI to follow. There's no code or anything technical—it's in Portuguese. Replace the parts between < >.

SKILL: Responder cliente no WhatsApp

QUANDO USAR: sempre que eu colar uma mensagem de cliente.

COMO FAZER (siga nesta ordem):
1. Cumprimente pelo nome usando <meu jeito, ex: "Oi, tudo bem?">.
2. Responda a dúvida em até <3 frases>, sem termos técnicos.
3. Termine com uma pergunta que ajude a fechar a venda.
4. Use o tom <ex: educado e próximo, sem ser formal demais>.

NÃO FAÇA: prometer prazo que eu não confirmei; usar emoji em excesso.

How to know it worked: when you paste a customer's message, the AI already replies in the your tone, the right length, and ending with a question—without you having to repeat the rules. That’s the skill in action.

✓ Good candidate for a skill

  • ✓A task you repeat the same way
  • ✓There's a format or tone that matters
  • ✓You can describe the step-by-step process

✗ No skill needed

  • ✗One question you'll never ask again
  • ✗A task that changes completely every time
  • ✗Something you don’t even know how you want yet

Key concepts

📌 Skill = ready-made recipe
📌 Your knowledge packaged
📌 Always the same, without re-explaining
📌 It’s just text, not programming
2

🔌 What Is MCP

The skill teaches AI to think your way. But thinking isn’t enough — it needs to act in the world: read your email, create a calendar event, update a spreadsheet. That’s what the MCP (Model Context Protocol), a “universal outlet” standard.

Before, connecting AI to each service was a a different workaround for each one. MCP solves this: it’s like the wall outlet standard — any device with the right plug simply plugs in. With MCP, AI plugs into email, spreadsheets, calendars the same way, without manual adaptation.

💡 Practical tip

You’re not going to “build” the outlet. In most cases, the MCP connector for the service you use already exists — you just turn it on. Remember the word MCP: when you hear “there’s an MCP for Gmail,” understand it as “you can connect AI to Gmail without a hacky workaround.”

Key concepts

📌 MCP = universal outlet
📌 Connects AI to real services
📌 One standard, no hacks
📌 Skill = think · MCP = act
3

🧰 Tools and connectors

Each connector it’s a cable that gives a new power for AI. A web connector lets it research. A file connector lets it read and write documents. An email connector lets it send messages. A database connector lets it query your records. The more connectors you have, the more things AI can actually do.

The fun part is that you put the kit together as needed, like a toolbox. Need to work with spreadsheets? Get the spreadsheet connector. Need to respond to customers? Get the WhatsApp or email one. You don't need to connect everything—just what solves your problem.

🌐
Web: the AI researches, reads pages, and brings back up-to-date information.
📁
Files: it opens, edits, and creates documents on your computer.
📲
Apps: email, calendar, WhatsApp—it works within your apps.
🗄️
Databases: it checks and updates your records and contact lists.

💡 Practical tip

Before plugging anything in, ask: "what capability is the AI missing to solve my pain point?". If your pain point is answering email, the email connector is worth more than ten others. A lean kit beats a full one.

Key concepts

📌 Connector = new power
📌 Web, files, apps, databases
📌 You put together the kit
📌 Only what solves your pain point
4

🌐 The ecosystem

Here’s the best news: you aren’t alone in putting everything together from scratch. There is a ecosystem — a growing world of skills and connectors ready-made, made and shared by the community. Someone has already made a lot of what you need.

"Ecosystem" here just means this: a bunch of ready-made pieces that work together. You don’t create—you plugs in. It’s the difference between making your own charger and grabbing one that’s already in the drawer.

✓ What the ecosystem gives you

  • ✓Ready-made skills that you just adapt
  • ✓Ready-made connectors for popular apps
  • ✓Community testing and improving

✗ What you do NOT need to do

  • ✗Reinventing what already exists
  • ✗Program each connector from scratch
  • ✗Start everything on your own

Key concepts

📌 World of components ready
📌 Made by the community
📌 You plugs in, don't create
📌 Grow every day
5

💻 Why it becomes an "OS" (operating system)

Separately, these pieces are useful. Together, they become something else. When you combine memory (the AI remembers your context) + skills (knows how to do it your way) + connectors (acts on your services), the AI stops being a chat and becomes a central hub that controls everything.

That’s exactly the role of the operating system (the "OS," like Windows or Android): the base program that coordinates all the others and organizes the computer. With this complete kit, the AI becomes this command center. And this is the foundation of Jarvis that you’ll build in Track 3.

📊 The account that becomes an "Operating System"

  • •Memory — the AI remembers who you are and what you’ve already agreed on.
  • •+ Skills — it knows how to carry out tasks your way.
  • •+ Connectors — it acts in your apps and services.
  • •= Command center — an AI that coordinates everything, like an OS.

Memory is the topic of the next module (1.4). For now, it’s enough to understand that the combination of these pieces creates the leap.

💡 Practical tip

Don’t try to sense the “SO” on day one. It emerges as you build up the pieces. Start with one skill and one connector; the “command center” feeling comes naturally as your toolkit grows.

Key concepts

📌 Memory + skills + connectors
📌 AI becomes command center
📌 OS = coordinates everything
📌 Foundation of the Jarvis (Track 3)
6

🧱 Building your toolkit

The trap for beginners is wanting to put everything together at once — ten skills, twenty connectors — and getting stuck before using any of them. The approach that works is the opposite: start small. ONE skill + ONE connector that solve one specific pain point of yours.

When this first pair works, you gain the confidence and clarity to add the next one. The kit grows out of real necessity, not out of anxiety. That's how you avoid getting stuck.

1

🎯 Choose ONE pain point

The tedious task that takes up most of your time today.

2

📜 Write ONE Skill

The recipe for how you solve that pain point, in Portuguese.

3

🔌 Grab ONE Connector

The only service the AI needs to access to get the job done.

4

✅ Use it, then scale up

Did it work? Now you can add the next piece.

🎯 The pattern to watch for

A kit that grows out of necessity is a kit you'll use. A kit assembled out of anxiety is a kit you'll abandon. One useful asset today is worth more than ten unused ones.
